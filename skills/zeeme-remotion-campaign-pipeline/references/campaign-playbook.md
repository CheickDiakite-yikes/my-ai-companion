# ZeeMe Campaign Playbook

## Narrative Arc (45s)
1. Hook (0-2s): visual punch + identity cue.
2. Promise (2-12s): "I'm here, I listen."
3. Product flow (12-32s): voice, text, continuity, personalization.
4. Emotional payoff (32-40s): companionship + growth.
5. CTA (40-45s): start now.

## Motion Direction
- Prefer smooth, low-jitter transitions over abrupt cuts.
- Keep orb and particle motion readable at mobile sizes.
- Use easing to make scene changes feel premium.

## Copy and Layout Rules
- Primary headline: <= 3 lines on 320px width.
- Keep subtitle to one concise line when possible.
- Avoid dense blocks near bottom CTA zone.
- Maintain high contrast over gradients.

## Social Deliverables
- 9:16: primary ad + story format.
- 1:1: feed-safe variant.
- 4:5: feed conversion format.
- Generate at least 3 still thumbnails for A/B tests.

## Review Checklist
- First frame and first 2 seconds clearly branded.
- CTA text visible without zoom.
- No clipped text at safe margins.
- Export filenames deterministic for handoff.
